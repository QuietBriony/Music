# Boundaries

This OS is meant to keep lyric work alive without putting unsafe or premature
material into the repo.

## Hard No

Do not commit:

- finished lyrics
- raw lyric drafts intended for release
- voice memos
- m4a/mp3/wav files
- sample URLs
- copied artist lines
- copied melody or motif descriptions close enough to recreate a song
- private family details beyond metadata anchors already approved here

## No Representative Claim

Do not write as:

- the voice of Okinawa
- the voice of all Uchinanchu
- a definitive Ryukyu position
- a clean independence slogan
- a moral lecturer

Allowed stance:

- "one local life looking sideways"
- "someone who cannot look away"
- "someone who does not want to cheapen the word"
- "a working adult holding scene, family, capital, and ancestor pressure"

## No Tourist Okinawa

Avoid easy postcard imagery unless the user explicitly reintroduces it for a
specific reason.

Default avoid:

- beach as shorthand
- port as shorthand
- blue sea as shorthand
- island paradise
- healing resort mood
- generic tropical color

## No Slogan Collapse

Political material should not collapse into:

- "基地反対" as the whole line
- "独立" as a hook without hesitation
- "日本が悪い" as the whole diagnosis
- "アメリカが悪い" as the whole diagnosis
- simple victim/hero frames

Instead, force the line through daily life: work, road, grave, household,
company, risk, body, night, machine, and weather.
