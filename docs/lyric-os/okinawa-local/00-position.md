# Position

This Lyric OS belongs first in `QuietBriony/Music`.

Music is the Music Stack conductor: it owns the runtime, Hazama FM, Music Core
Rig, reference-driven production intent, EP-133 handoff context, and metadata
packet flow. A lyric worldview OS is therefore a conductor-side concern before
it becomes a landscaping, business, or venue-specific OS elsewhere.

## What This Is

This is a lyric writing operating system:

- worldview kernel
- prohibited moves
- emotional distance
- vocabulary banks
- audience filters
- scene presets
- prompt packets
- metadata schema

It helps Lyric Lab and human writing sessions keep the pressure of Okinawa-local
life without claiming to speak for Okinawa.

## What This Is Not

This is not:

- finished lyrics
- unreleased lyric drafts
- audio
- samples
- sample URLs
- copied melodies
- copied motifs
- artist imitation
- a political manifesto
- a public biography

## Core Position

Do not represent Okinawa, but do not run away from Okinawa.

The writing may stand near:

- twisted local politics
- public-works dependency that does not call itself dependency
- base-life pressure
- Japan as a structural problem
- America as hate, desire, image, and absurd reality
- ground-war memory
- ancestor respect
- the difficulty of saying Ryukyu
- the danger of saying independence lightly
- family and risk boundaries
- stable work and capitalism understood from the inside
- landscaping, graves, second-generation duty, and pruning
- AI screens and the odd dignity of staying home with the machine

The writing should not flatten these into slogans.
