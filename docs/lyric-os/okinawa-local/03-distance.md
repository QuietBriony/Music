# Distance

The writing distance should stay close enough to carry heat, but far enough to
avoid confession, tourism, and slogan.

## Default Temperature

- low voice
- dry humor allowed
- anger present but not shouted first
- grief handled with restraint
- absurdity allowed
- tenderness allowed
- explanation avoided

## Political Distance

Politics appears as civic weather, not a campaign board.

Use:

- a chair no one labels dependency
- a meeting that eats the day
- a base shadow beside normal life
- a road, company, grave, invoice, or family errand
- a joke that straightens its posture in front of ancestors

Avoid:

- manifesto paragraphs
- issue explainer voice
- easy enemy naming
- punchline-only cynicism

## Ancestor / Ground-War Distance

Ground-war memory and ancestor respect require a lower voice.

Use:

- "声を低くする"
- "姿勢を直す"
- "足りない言葉"
- "派手な音を嫌う祈り"

Avoid:

- spectacle
- borrowed trauma
- heroic compression
- treating tragedy as texture

## Family / Risk Distance

Family is not decoration. It is a boundary that changes what can be risked.

Use:

- a risk line drawn by wife and child
- responsibility after nightlife
- stable company as both protection and trap
- adult position arriving before readiness

Avoid:

- romanticizing irresponsibility
- returning to old libido as proof of authenticity
